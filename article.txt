The Interpreter centers on the conflict and coexistence between the AI interpreter “AnPlus” and human simultaneous interpreters. Pre-translation involved a contrastive analysis of source and target cultures to determine the positioning of the target text. During translation, a dynamic balance between domestication and foreignization was maintained. Core metaphors and emotional undertones were preserved, while technical terminology and the protagonist’s internal monologue were appropriately adapted to ensure cross-cultural acceptability while retaining narrative tension.
Translating this text requires not only the accurate conveyance of specialized terms and complex narrative structures, but also flexible sentence restructuring to suit linguistic norms. To achieve this, the translator adopts a combination of literal translation and free translation, based on the demands of different contexts.
